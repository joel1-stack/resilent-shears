About Resilient Shears

Our story and commitment to beauty

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Resilient Shears has grown from a small neighborhood salon to a premier beauty destination at Waruku Behind Car Wash. Our journey has been marked by dedication to quality, affordability, and customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional beauty services that enhance our clients' natural beauty while offering a relaxing and rejuvenating experience. We believe everyone deserves to look and feel their best without breaking the bank.

Why Choose Us

  • Professional and certified stylists
  • High-quality products
  • Affordable pricing
  • Clean and hygienic environment
  • Flexible working hours (7am-10pm)
